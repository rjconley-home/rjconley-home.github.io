Family Hub privacy policy

Last updated November 13, 2026.

Family Hub is a private app used by one family in its own home. It is not offered to the public.

What it uses

With the family's permission, Family Hub reads and updates the family's own Google Calendar so events can be shown and added on a screen at home.

Where the data goes

Calendar information is kept only on the family's own computer at home. It is not sold, shared, or sent to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's Security settings, under third-party apps with account access. Disconnecting in Family Hub also deletes its copy of the calendar.

Family Hub's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
